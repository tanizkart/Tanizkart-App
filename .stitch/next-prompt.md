High-fidelity mobile Category & Warehouse Aisle Explorer Screen for 'Tanizkart' warehouse quick-commerce app ('Save money save time').
Device: Mobile (390px fluid layout).

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
Brand Theme: Royal Gold Quick-Commerce (Blinkit & Instamart Grade).
Colors:
- Primary: Electric Royal Blue (#0052CC) for headers, active tabs, and category badges
- Secondary: Warm Golden Amber (#F5A623) for discount tags, CTA accents, and highlights
- Tertiary: Rapid Emerald (#10B981) for SLA speed badges ("⚡ 10 MINS")
- Background Canvas: Crisp light slate (#F8FAFC)
- Card Surfaces: Pure White (#FFFFFF) with subtle shadows (border-radius: 16px to 20px)
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular rupee pricing (₹)

**Page Structure:**
1. Top Sticky Header with back arrow to Home, search input ("Search in 24+ warehouse categories..."), and Cart pill with badge.
2. Vertical Left Rail (Master Categories):
   - Dairy, Bread & Eggs (Active with blue indicator)
   - Fruits & Vegetables
   - Snacks & Munchies
   - Cold Drinks & Juices
   - Atta, Rice, Oil & Dals
   - Instant & Frozen Food
   - Household & Cleaning
   - Personal Care
3. Right Scrollable Content Pane (Subcategories & Featured SKUs):
   - Visual subcategory banner with high-impact appetizing graphics
   - Grid of subcategory tiles with zoomed-in 85% fill close-up packshots
   - "Popular in this Aisle" mini-shelf with real prices and gold stepper counters
4. Floating Bottom Cart Pill (3 items • ₹187) and Shared Bottom Navigation Bar (Categories active).
