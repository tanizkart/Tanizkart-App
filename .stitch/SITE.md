# Tanizkart Site Constitution & Roadmap

## 1. Project Vision & Identity
**Tanizkart** is a premier warehouse-based quick-commerce mobile application delivering groceries, daily staples, fresh produce, dairy, snacks, drinks, household goods, and personal care directly from local dark stores in 10–15 minutes.
- **Tagline**: *"Save money save time"*
- **Official Brand Mark**: Glossy 3D Blue Shopping Cart, circular yellow exchange arrows, Royal Blue '**TANIZ**' (`#0052CC`), 3D Golden Amber '**KART**' (`#F5A623`), and tagline *"Save money save time"*.
- **Aesthetic Benchmark**: Blinkit & Swiggy Instamart high-energy, mouth-watering quick commerce with close-up appetizing items and clean white surfaces.
- **Delivery Zone**: Lawyerpet, Ongole, Andhra Pradesh (kept subtle and consumer-centric).

## 3. Core Design Principles
1. **Electric Royal Blue & Golden Amber**: Grounded in the official brand logo—Royal Blue (`#0052CC`) anchors headers, active tabs, and primary accents; Vibrant Golden Amber (`#F5A623`) powers high-converting CTAs, `+ ADD` buttons, and discount chips.
2. **Appetizing Close-Up Product Visuals**: Products are zoomed in, filling 80–90% of cards with soft shadows, making groceries look tangible, delicious, and fresh like top Dribbble delivery apps.
3. **Consumer-Friendly Delivery Navigation**: Sleek `⚡ 10 MINS` speed badge + `Deliver to Flat 302, Lawyerpet ▾` dropdown without heavy repetitive dark store spam.
4. **Luminous Crisp Surfaces**: Pure white rounded cards (`border-radius: 18px`) floating over soft slate canvas (`#F8FAFC`).
5. **Fluid Thumb Navigation**: Connected flow across Home ➔ Product Listing ➔ Product Detail ➔ Cart/Checkout ➔ Live Tracking & Profile.
